# Home contracts

The homepage: hero, lens and sonar, About and the section cards.

Part of the site's behaviour contracts: recorded UX decisions that are intentional constraints. Read the README's [Owner preferences](../../README.md#owner-preferences) and [Dead ends](../../README.md#dead-ends-and-hidden-dependencies) first, and [site-wide.md](site-wide.md) for anything shared. Moved word for word from the README on 26 Nov 2026.

- Keep the scroll animation and background canvas.
- Canvas (owner-approved): particle network plus, every few seconds, one signal hopping up to four links, lighting nodes, resting ~2s; clipped out of the hero text (name to CTA).
- Text fade (13 Nov 2026, owner: "Yes, fix them"): as each frame's last step, everything on the canvas, the network included, is erased toward the hero text box with a soft edge (rounded steps every 8px over 64px, 40px on phones). No network line runs between the letters, the tagline or the buttons, and the lens glass never ends in a hard line where it is clipped.
- Hunt lens (11 Nov 2026, owner: "push A", from the index-motion mockups in `lab/index-motion/`): a faint lens follows the pointer and reveals a dim mesh of points and links hidden elsewhere (about 15% lift, slight bulge); when the pointer rests, a point inside flickers amber, a signal runs two links to it, and it settles teal with a soft ring. No labels. With no pointer (phones, or before the mouse moves) the lens drifts on its own (phones: the band below the buttons, the whole lens 16px inside every screen edge since 27 Nov 2026, approved from `lab/visual-ideas` board 9, where it drifted to 0.55 of its radius from the sides and 0.35 from the bottom; a tap moves it). Lens drawing is clipped out of the hero text box like the signals; it fades before its rim reaches the text. The canvas is scaled for high-density screens (capped at 2x).
- Threats and sonar (11 Nov 2026 evening, owner: "port A5", tab A5 in `lab/index-motion/`; replaces the lens's random find above). Lens size, glass and brightness are unchanged; owner rejected a bigger, brighter lens as "too big and glaring".
  - A few real threats (6 desktop, 4 phones) hide in the mesh, clear of the text, the edges and the desktop bar, and hold still. Through the lens each is a dim amber point.
  - Near one, the lens leans onto it; a 300ms rest finds it: it warms amber (no flicker), a signal walks two mesh links to it, and a teal ring closes. Found threats stay amber for the visit (owner: "the orange dots shouldnt disappear"); once all are found, one more surfaces every ~24s, up to 8 (5 on phones).
  - Each find, and the lens's first arrival (about 2.1s, as the headline settles), sends one faint wave that bursts out and eases off over 1.8s (1.5s phones), at most one per 4s. As it passes it shows the mesh in a band, blinks network nodes it crosses (never within 30px of the text) and blinks unfound threats amber for ~3s. No wavefront line: it was invisible, and a brighter one would glare.
  - The lens also fades before the desktop nav bar instead of sliding under it.
  - Dropped in review: warming the mesh near threats and an amber arc on the lens rim (did not read at 1x); joining finds with lines (long lines framed the name like a border).
- Staged entrance (same day): the name sharpens from a soft blur as one block (never per letter; first paint at 25% opacity so it still counts for LCP), the headline lands and scrambles as the name settles, then the support line and each CTA follow 120ms apart. CSS only, `!important` because main.css switches animations off until load; the summary is held off screen for 240ms so the scramble's own visible hold starts as the name lands. Reduced motion: all shown at once, no lens. It plays once per visit: `game.js` sets `sessionStorage.heroSeen`, and `page-transitions.js` (in `<head>`) adds `html.hero-seen`, which the entrance rules exclude, so a return to Home starts at rest and no blurred name ghosts the page transition (11 Nov 2026 review).
- Never propose again: labelled agent-graph canvas; letter-by-letter name rise (hurt LCP).
- Hero is `100lvh` so the phone opening stays black in both URL-bar states.
- Phones only: bottom padding absorbs `lvh` − `svh` to keep content centred; desktop is plain 4rem.
- `initHeroSnap` (`game.js`): a scroll settling mid-band glides on to About (900ms, ease-in-out). Phones land on its measured document top, finishing the portrait sweep; wider screens (owner, 10 Nov 2026: "desktop should glide too") land where the stage's pin ends, photo, copy and all three cards settled, or at the page's end if sooner. Touch, wheel, scroll keys or a scrollbar press cancel; a held finger or scrollbar press waits; other clicks, Shift and Tab leave a glide alone.
- Gate rules from the 10 Nov 2026 review (each fixed a measured bounce or wrong landing): a glide follows the browser's own smooth scroll (keys, return-to-top) and eases on from where it stops, never pulling against it; only scrolls that follow wheel, key, touch or scrollbar input (momentum included) count as the reader's, and anything else (a link, find-in-page, focus, a restored scroll after reload or history) settles on the nearer frame; a move under 24px off a frame springs back (400ms); a width change mid-glide re-aims at the new frame.
- Measure About's real offset, not hero height, or the glide falls short.
- Desktop home (981px and up; owner, 10 Nov 2026: "the header bar is on neither resting frame"): the hero is `100vh` less the bar's 3.5rem (3.25rem until 15 Nov 2026), so the bar runs along the first screen's foot; the bar is sticky at the top, opaque `#030708` (the 0.36 teal over black), and `measure()` centres the About cluster in the space under it, so both resting frames carry the bar.
