# Personal contracts

The Personal timeline and its House and Pre-wedding pages.

Part of the site's behaviour contracts: recorded UX decisions that are intentional constraints. Read the README's [Owner preferences](../../README.md#owner-preferences) and [Dead ends](../../README.md#dead-ends-and-hidden-dependencies) first, and [site-wide.md](site-wide.md) for anything shared. Moved word for word from the README on 26 Nov 2026.

- Latte and Mocha links to `@twoshotsofcuteness` (Instagram icon); never restore popover, mockup or embed.
- Orientation and Naval Diving Unit are self-contained galleries, not popups or pages; linked-gallery hover, default cursor. Orientation rotates two real photos, NDU three; never duplicate frames. `experience_ndu` stays unpublished.
- Personal card, pre-wedding and house images need authored `sizes` and `-480`/`-800` siblings:
  - Personal: `(max-width: 736px) and (min-resolution: 2.5dppx) 266px, (max-width: 736px) 85vw, (max-width: 1680px) 234px, 312px` (15 Nov 2026, from 275px/366px: the cards' photos are 215-234px to 1680px and 312px above, so 2x laptops take `-480`; 1001 to 439 KB of card photos at 1440 DPR 2)
  - Galleries: `(max-width: 736px) and (min-resolution: 2.5dppx) 266px, (max-width: 736px) 90vw, (max-width: 767px) 76vw, (max-width: 1023px) 40vw, (max-width: 1279px) 27vw, 20vw`
- DPR-3 clause caps phones at `-800`. House siblings sit beside the WebP overlay in `images-webp/personal/house/`, because the build looks for siblings next to the resolved file. `verify.ps1` checks slots and siblings. Pre-wedding phones stay on `-800` deliberately. The NDU card got its `-480` siblings and the Personal card `sizes` in Nov 2026; `ndu2` (a 150 KB 576px JPEG) ships as a q90 WebP overlay in `images-webp/experience/NDU/` with its `-480` beside it (49 KB, SSIM .99 to the source). The Europe banner (1280px, under `--banners-only`'s 1400px floor) has a hand-made `switzerland-1200.webp` (q82, the same SSIM as a q82 JPEG tier, 27% under the original) so DPR-3 phones skip the 214 KB original.
- Mobile: endpoints inside the viewport, larger card type, return-to-top hidden.
- The five timeline galleries have no photo controls (owner decision, 17 Oct 2026).
- Desktop crossfades on hover/keyboard focus, 1s per photo. Touch plays only the card nearest reading focus, 4s per photo. Reduced motion holds first photos.
- Cats, house, pre-wedding rotate five photos; crossfades overlap.
- Use animation longhands (shorthand resets `animation-delay`).
- On touch, later frames stay `display: none` until `warmGallery` marks the card warm, once it is on screen, ahead of activation and the first crossfade. Since 6 Nov 2026 `warmGallery` loads the first frame at high priority and frames 3-5 at low through a detached image, handing their sources back (and showing them) once that arrives: laid out, even hidden or at opacity 0, Chrome raised every hidden frame to high priority alongside the visible photos. The second frame loads in place (7 Nov 2026): it is due two seconds after activation, and handed back through the detached image it faded to an empty frame on slow 4G and Chrome logged its fade-in as a new, larger LCP (the opened Cats card, 2.1 s to 5.6 s). A frame still loading when its fade-in starts pauses the whole slideshow (`animationstart`, `is-awaiting-frame`) until that photo arrives or fails, so the crossfade waits instead of fading to nothing.
- Per-image `--focus-x`/`--focus-y`; no per-class `object-position`. `house_1` sits at `--focus-y: 70%` so the photographer's mark stays out of the steady frame; since 15 Nov 2026 the House card's frames are also scaled 1.12 from the bottom centre (`scale`, so the slot is unchanged, though the image is drawn about 12% larger than its `sizes` says, an 8% upscale at 2x, accepted), because the mark still showed in the desktop tile. The Brawl Stars card was taken off the timeline (owner, 9 Nov 2026), and off Projects the same day.
- Frames target 3:2 where they can: every desktop card has `--image-col` 14.5rem and a 10.5rem photo floor; phones (520px and below) use 16/9. Latte & Mocha and Code in the Community use `personal-event-card--upright` to keep a squarer frame (11rem on desktop, 3/2 on phones; widening crops them harder).
- Measure before retuning ratios; the timeline column caps cards at ~451px, not `max-width`.
- Desktop photo sets a card-height floor, never a ceiling; frames absolutely positioned.
- Cards never change size with scroll position (owner, 9 Nov 2026: scrolling was "very clunky"). The old fold/unfold moved on-screen cards by themselves on 40% of desktop frames (layout shift 0.42 desktop, 0.92 phone); steady cards measure 0 for both. Focus only outlines, lifts and dims: opacity, transform, filter, border and shadow, never width, height, padding, margin, aspect ratio or `min-height`. Phones dim every card but the one being read; desktop dims only read cards; at the top and foot of the page every card on screen is undimmed. Cards start their entrance as soon as any of them is on screen. Phone cards in one year sit 1.25rem apart. Desktop cards in one year overlap by 2.5rem, so their rail dots sit about 130px apart against about 216px across years (9 Nov 2026; at 4.75rem each year's pair bunched at 94px). The card being read has a filled, ringed rail dot (`is-focus::before`), since desktop leaves unread cards undimmed and up to three can look current. Focus sticky by 48px. `personal-timeline.js` never writes scroll. A scroll-driven `animation-timeline: view()` opacity fade was tried instead of the class dim and dropped: about 4x the long frames on a phone viewport.
- `data-category` is structural; no category colours or legend.
- A card whose caption only restated its title has no caption (17 Nov 2026: Pre-Wedding Photoshoot, the only one; House and Naval Diving Unit each add a fact and keep theirs). It carries `personal-event-card--title-only`, which closes the gap so meta, title and link are evenly spaced; never write a caption the owner has not supplied.
- Card captions (the line under each title) are in the reading serif, Merriweather 300 in `--color-ink-600` (27 Nov 2026, owner-approved type review): they were `system-ui`, so Segoe UI on Windows and San Francisco on Apple devices. Two captions run one line longer than in the system font (CAPTs_Lock on phones, Lightsaber on laptops). The Pre-Wedding page's script wordmark is set upright (`font-style: normal` in `prewed.html`): its font has no italic, so the theme's italic paragraph made Chrome slant it.
