28 June 2023 – 8 July 2023

Eleven days from Fremantle and Rottnest Island to Kalbarri, the Pinnacles, and Perth.

Wednesday, 28 June

Day 1 – Arrival in Fremantle

Route
Singapore Changi AirportTerminal 1→Perth AirportTerminal 1→Airport Central StationAirport Line→Perth StationPerth CBD→Fremantle StationFremantle→Quest FremantlePakenham Street

We reached Perth International Airport after a 5-hour flight from Singapore. First thing we did was get a Transperth SmartRider card — basically their EZ-Link. From there, we made our way to Fremantle, although we encountered a slight confusion regarding the correct stop to disembark while changing lines. Eventually, we realised that trains from different lines shared the same platform but had staggered schedules. After approximately an hour, we finally reached Fremantle, only to notice that the majority of stores had already closed by 5:30 PM.

We spent the whole of 29 June exploring Fremantle. Our accommodation near the coast made it convenient to reach all the places we wanted to visit. Our first stop was Bathers Bay, where we were greeted by a multitude of birds—Xian's sworn enemies, as it turned out! Since the Fremantle Market was closed on Thursdays, we decided to explore other tourist attractions in the area. We made our way to the WA Maritime Museum and Fremantle Prison, both worth the visit. As tourists, we made sure to visit some Instagram-worthy spots.

Friday, 30 June

Day 3 – Rottnest Island

Route
B Shed Ferry TerminalFremantle→Rottnest IslandWadjemup→Bickley BayRottnest Island→Porpoise BayRottnest Island→Wadjemup LighthouseRottnest Island→The SettlementRottnest Island→Fremantle MarketsFremantle→PerthWestern Australia

Celebrating Xian's birthday on Rottnest Island was one of my favourite days. We took a ferry from Fremantle to reach the island, purchasing our tickets from Rottnest Express, which also included bike rentals. Exploring Rottnest Island on bicycles was a delightful way to discover its beauty. They say it's home to the largest Quokka population in Australia, and we had the pleasure of spotting many of these adorable creatures. We even had the chance to feed one of them with leaves. After bidding farewell to Rottnest Island, we headed back to Fremantle.

Day 4 – Caversham & Swan Valley

Route
PerthWestern Australia→Caversham Wildlife ParkWhiteman Park→House of HoneySwan Valley→Swan Settlers MarketHerne Hill→Mondo NougatSwan Valley→Margaret River Chocolate CompanySwan Valley→PerthWestern Australia

Today we visited Caversham Wildlife Park and got up close with the koalas and kangaroos. Xian fed a kangaroo and her hands were filled with remnants of its saliva. She also got chased by baby goats and a huge turkey. The interactive farm show was entertaining and educational. We witnessed the fascinating process of sheep getting their wool shaved and participated in the milking of cows.

Monday, 3 July

Day 6 – The Pinnacles

Route
PerthWestern Australia→Lake ThetisCervantes→Lobster ShackCervantes→Pinnacles DesertNambung National Park→GeraldtonMid West

Our journey began in the morning from Perth, for our three-day trip. Our first stop was Lake Thetis, home to the intriguing stromatolites, Earth’s living fossils. Next, we had lunch at Lobster Shack. Xian ate both the lobsters and that was her best meal in Perth. Feeling full and satisfied (especially Xian), we continued our journey to Nambung National Park - Pinnacles Desert. We wandered around the desert and took many photos by using the limestone formations as a phone stand.

Day 8 – Greenough & Lancelin

Route
GeraldtonMid West→HMAS Sydney II MemorialGeraldton→Central GreenoughGreenough→Central Greenough CafeGreenough→Lancelin Sand DunesLancelin→PerthWestern Australia

On our way back to Perth, we made a pit stop in Greenough, and strolled through the historical village. We went past the HMAS Sydney II Memorial and braved the rain to take some photos there. For lunch, we stopped by the Central Greenough Cafe. There were many Alpacas there and we got to feed them. Ending the three-day trip, we went sandboarding at the Lancelin Sand Dunes - the wind there was super strong.

Friday, 7 July – Saturday, 8 July

Day 10 – Scitech, Shopping & Departure

Route
Rydges Perth Kings SquarePerth CBD→ScitechWest Perth→Watertown Brand Outlet CentreWest Perth→Perth AirportTerminal 1→SingaporeHome

In the morning, we visited Scitech, a science centre. It turned out to be more for kids. We then proceeded to Watertown Outlet Mall for some shopping, where we picked up shoes and clothes. Afterward, we returned to our hotel and headed to the airport. Along the way, we made a stop at another outlet mall, DFO Perth. Unfortunately, there wasn't much to see as most items were too expensive, and many shops were closing. To make matters worse, it started raining on our way there. With heavy but satisfied hearts, we began our journey back to Singapore.
